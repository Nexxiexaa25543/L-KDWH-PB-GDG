── Keep on Smiling ──

Even if it means getting hurt, being trampled on over and over until I'm dead, I'm willing to bear it all. Maybe it would be better if little me just died. But... but one thing's for certain: little me will never die because of this brokenness. I'll keep smiling no matter what...

Since Nex is such a coward, way too sensitive, and far too afraid to talk about his real life, I guess I'll let this other version of him speak instead—the one who can usually express himself quite fluently online.

He's always been someone who likes helping others. Whenever he sees someone suffering, he can't help but feel uneasy about it. On top of that, he's extremely sensitive. Like, ridiculously sensitive. I don't know what makes him so emotionally fragile, honestly. Remember when he said he'd faint just from seeing human organs? Yeah, that's because of how sensitive he is. Though, I guess that part is understandable.

Whenever someone insults him or directs harsh words at him, he starts overthinking everything. Sometimes, he even cries where nobody can see him. Whenever he treats his own mother badly, he ends up blaming himself so much afterward. He hates himself for it, wondering, "Why the hell did I do that?" And sometimes, he ends up hurting himself because of all that self-hatred. He wants to apologize for what he did, but he doesn't have the courage to say it because of his own shortcomings.

He's just tired of life, both online and in the real world. Online, he's encountered all kinds of people, each different from the next. But most of the time, he seems to run into people struggling with depression or carrying terrible things from their past. He doesn't even know what he might have done in a past life to keep meeting so many people like that.

Even so, he always tries to encourage others. He carefully thinks through every word he says, trying to find the most appropriate and meaningful things to say so he can comfort them in the best way possible. But no matter how many people he comforts, nobody really knows just how hard he has to force himself to smile. He's never let anyone see his sad side. He's chosen not to talk about it because he doesn't want anyone to worry about him.

He wonders, "Suffering? What's that? Can you eat it?" The word happiness feels foreign to him. He's come to hate his own existence, to hate the life he's living. And yet, no matter how much he hates it... "I will fight for it." "I will struggle." "I will laugh and be cheerful." "I will grieve and feel sorrow." "My life. MY life. MY LIFE. I MUST KEEP LIVING."

Let me tell you a little about his past. Back in elementary school, he was bullied all the damn time. But he kept standing tall, forcing himself to smile through it all. They bullied him, hurled insults at him, and treated him terribly. And the worst part? They even forced him into a boxing match.

His emotions swing around so much that they feel like a damn roller coaster. He doesn't even know whether he's an introvert, an ambivert, or an extrovert. When he's around other people, he forces himself to act cheerful as usual. But when he's alone, he's incredibly quiet. Sometimes, he doesn't feel like talking to a single soul.

Eventually, everything starts getting worse. He's like a tree with roots buried deep in the pine forest, surrounded by nothing but stress. There's the mountain of unfinished assignments, his relationships with other people, and his body, which seems to be wearing down a little more every day. It's incredibly difficult to explain, but comforting him isn't easy. Perhaps it's because he's always been the one comforting others. Whenever someone tries to comfort him, he becomes as stubborn and unyielding as a bag of rocks.

"Is something wrong? I'm worried about youuu. If anything's bothering you, you can tell me, okay?" Blah, blah, blah. Those are the kinds of messages people keep sending him. But to him, they're nothing more than empty, sugar-coated words—just another pretty little song about how wonderful the world is supposed to be.

Even though he's a complete mess and can barely express himself properly in real life, this other version of him is serious about what he says. Sometimes, he wonders whether he even deserves to have any friends at all. Maybe staying with his family is enough. Thank you for always staying by his side.

But in the end, sooner or later, we will all face our own breaking point. We are born, and someday, we will die. I am no different from you. Eventually, we will all pass away, saying our final goodbyes to one another. But until that day comes... "We, we, WE will take this one life we've been given, hold tightly to who we are, embrace our own existence, and keep moving forward."
